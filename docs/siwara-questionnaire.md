# SIWARA — Brand Building Questionnaire

## 1. THE BASICS
*The foundation — we need this before anything else*

**1.1 What is Siwara?**
Women's fashion? Modest wear? Luxury? Streetwear? Sportswear? Accessories? All of the above?

**1.2 Who is it for?**
Age range? Income level? Lifestyle?
Example: "25-40 year old professional women in the Gulf who want modest but stylish everyday wear"

**1.3 What makes Siwara different from other brands?**
Why would someone buy from you instead of Zara/H&M/Shein? What's the ONE thing you want people to remember?

**1.4 Price range?**
Budget (under 200 AED), mid-range (200-500), premium (500-1500), luxury (1500+)?

**1.5 Where is the business registered?**
UAE? Saudi? UK? Other? Trade license number if you have one.

---

## 2. THE BRAND STORY
*This is what makes people FEEL something about your brand*

**2.1 Who is behind Siwara?**
Your wife's name (or if she prefers to stay anonymous). Her background — what did she do before this? Why fashion? What's her personal connection to it?

**2.2 Where does the name "Siwara" come from?**
Does it mean something? Is it a word from a language? Any personal significance?

**2.3 What's the origin story?**
Every great brand has a moment: "I couldn't find X, so I decided to make it myself." What's yours?

**2.4 What does Siwara stand for?**
3-5 values. Examples: craftsmanship, sustainability, empowerment, heritage, minimalism, confidence.

**2.5 Is there a tagline or slogan?**
Example: SKN uses "Soft as Skin. Strong as You." If not, we'll create one — but any ideas?

---

## 3. THE PRODUCTS
*We need this to build the store*

**3.1 What products will you launch with?**
List every item with:
- Name
- Description (2-3 sentences)
- Price
- Available sizes
- Available colors
- Material/fabric

**3.2 Do you have product photos already?**
If yes: send them all (the more the better). If no: do you have samples we can photograph? Or do you want AI-generated product visuals for now?

**3.3 How many products at launch?**
Starting with 5 items? 20? 50?

**3.4 Will you add new products regularly?**
Monthly drops? Seasonal collections? As you go?

**3.5 Are products made by you or sourced?**
Designed in-house and manufactured? Private label? Where is production? (Turkey, China, local, etc.)

---

## 4. THE LOOK & FEEL
*This determines how the website, packaging, and everything visual will look*

**4.1 Send me 5-10 websites you LOVE the look of.**
Fashion brands, any brands — just things that make you think "I want my brand to feel like THIS." (SKN Sportswear is one reference — send more)

**4.2 What colors represent Siwara?**
Pick 2-3 main colors. Not sure? Tell me the MOOD:
- Warm & earthy (beige, sand, terracotta)
- Cool & minimal (white, gray, black)
- Bold & vibrant (red, emerald, royal blue)
- Dark & luxurious (black, gold, deep green)

**4.3 What FEELING should someone get visiting your site?**
Examples: "luxury boutique", "approachable and fun", "clean and minimal", "edgy and bold", "heritage and craftsmanship"

**4.4 Do you have a logo?**
If yes: send the file (preferably PNG with transparent background + the original design file if possible). If no: we'll design one. Any preferences?

**4.5 Typography preference?**
- Serif (elegant, traditional): like Vogue
- Sans-serif (modern, clean): like Zara
- Script (feminine, flowing): like Victoria's Secret
- Arabic calligraphy included? (bilingual logo?)

---

## 5. OPERATIONS
*The boring stuff that makes the business work*

**5.1 Where do you ship?**
UAE only? GCC? Worldwide? List specific countries if limited.

**5.2 Shipping costs?**
Free shipping? Free above a certain amount? Flat rate? Varies by location?

**5.3 Return policy?**
How many days? Who pays return shipping? Exchange only or full refund?

**5.4 Payment methods?**
Credit/debit card? Apple Pay? Cash on delivery? Tabby/Tamara (buy now pay later)?

**5.5 Currency?**
AED? SAR? USD? Multiple?

**5.6 Do you have a warehouse/storage?**
Where do orders ship from? How fast can you ship? (same day, 1-2 days, 3-5 days?)

---

## 6. CONTENT & SOCIAL
*This builds your audience and drives sales*

**6.1 Does Siwara have social media accounts yet?**
Instagram handle? TikTok? Pinterest? If not, what handles do you want? (check availability!)

**6.2 Will the founder be the face of the brand?**
Some brands show the person, some stay anonymous. Does your wife want to appear in content?

**6.3 Do you have any photo/video content already?**
Lookbook? Behind-the-scenes? Fabric sourcing? Even phone photos are useful.

**6.4 Who is your dream customer?**
Describe ONE specific person who would buy Siwara. Her name, age, job, where she lives, what she drives, what other brands she buys, where she hangs out online. (This helps us target everything perfectly)

---

## 7. COMPETITOR RESEARCH
*Know thy enemy*

**7.1 Who are your 3-5 closest competitors?**
Brands selling similar products at similar prices to similar customers. Links to their websites.

**7.2 What do they do WELL that you want to match?**

**7.3 What do they do BADLY that you want to do better?**

---

## 8. LEGAL & DOMAIN
*The technical bits*

**8.1 Do you own a domain name?**
siwara.com? siwara.ae? siwara.sa? If not, which do you want? (we'll check availability)

**8.2 Do you have a trade license?**
Which jurisdiction? UAE? Saudi? If not, are you planning to get one? (Some payment providers require it)

**8.3 Do you need Arabic language on the site?**
English only? English + Arabic? Arabic first?

---

## 9. BUDGET & TIMELINE

**9.1 When do you want to launch?**
ASAP? Specific date? Tied to an event?

**9.2 Budget range for the website?**
We need to know so we can scope appropriately. Ballpark is fine.

**9.3 Will you manage the store yourself after launch?**
Or do you want ongoing management? (product updates, content, marketing)

---

## 10. ANYTHING ELSE

**10.1 Is there anything I didn't ask that you think is important for Siwara?**

**10.2 Any hard deadlines or events coming up?**
(Ramadan collection, National Day launch, etc.)

**10.3 Send me EVERYTHING you have** — mood boards, sketches, fabric swatches, Instagram saves, Pinterest boards, voice notes describing your vision. The more I have, the better the result.
